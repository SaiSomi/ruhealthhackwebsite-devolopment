2025 Rutgers Healthcare Hackathon

Come join us on Oct 10-12, 2025 for the Rutgers Health Hack 2025 at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital and Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School in New Brunswick, NJ. We are excited to have cloud computing, AI, LLM, and many other resources and technology support from industry leaders including Amazon Web Services, Oracle, Microsoft, Nokia Bell Labs, Google Cloud, and Sevaro. No prior hacking experience necessary. Event is open to all including medical sciences, computer science, engineering, business, administration, design, law, and policy.

Keynote Speaker – October 10, 6:00 PM

Dr. Jared Saul, MD

Chief Medical Officer, AWS

Dr. Saul serves as the Chief Medical Officer for commercial healthcare and life sciences at Amazon Web Services (AWS) where he is focused on working with customers and partners to apply Amazon’s technology to advance human health. Before joining AWS, Dr. Saul practiced neuroradiology and pursued various startup ventures, eventually transitioning from clinical practice to fully pursue his entrepreneurial interests in roles such as founder, CTO, advisor, and investor.

Dr. Saul earned his medical degree from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, completed a radiology residency there, and then did a neuroradiology fellowship at the University of Arizona. He currently resides in New Jersey with his wife and two sons.
